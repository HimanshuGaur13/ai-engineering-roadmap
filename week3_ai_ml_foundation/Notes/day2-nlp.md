1. What is NLP?
   Natural Language Processing : It is the field of AI that helps computers understand human language.
   Examples - ChatGPT, Google Translate, Grammarly, Search Engines

2. NLP Pipeline
   Text → Clean → Tokenize → Process → Model

3. Tokenization = Splitting text into smaller pieces called tokens.
4. Text Cleaning -Removes:special characters, emojis, punctuation, unwanted spaces
5. Stop Words- Common words removed to reduce noise.
6. Stemming- Root form extraction
7. Lemmatization- Converting words into meaningful dictionary form.
